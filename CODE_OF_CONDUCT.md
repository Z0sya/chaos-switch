# Code of conduct

Treat participants with respect. Critique work clearly and constructively. Make space for people with different backgrounds and experience levels. Harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualized attention, personal attacks and sharing private information without consent are unacceptable.

Contact a maintainer privately to report a concern. Avoid sharing personal information in public issues. Maintainers may remove harmful material or limit participation to protect an open and respectful project. Handle security reports using [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).
